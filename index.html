How It Works

From sensor to insight
in four steps.

Connect

Install sensors and edge devices on your machines. We support PLCs, analog signals, energy meters, and more — legacy or modern.

02

Data flows securely to our cloud platform via edge gateways. No data is ever lost — even during network interruptions.
